FAQs

Job tracking questions.

Related: Dispatch Board · Work Orders · Inspections & Checklists

Which statuses does a job go through?

Draft, Open, Approved, Scheduled, Assigned, Dispatched, En Route, Arrived, In Progress, Completed and Closed, with side states for Awaiting Approval, Paused, Awaiting Parts, Awaiting Customer and Cancelled.

Can a technician skip a step?

No. Each status only allows the next valid ones, so a job can’t jump from Assigned to Completed without being dispatched and started.

When does the SLA clock pause?

While the job is Awaiting Customer. It restarts when the job goes back to In Progress or is rescheduled.

Do technicians only see their own jobs?

Staff with technician access see the jobs assigned to them. Managers and owners see every job.

Is arrival time exact?

No. Arrival times are estimates and are always labeled that way to the customer.
