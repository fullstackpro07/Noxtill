FAQs

Smart inventory questions.

How does it know when I’ll run out?

From how fast each product has been selling across your channels, compared with what’s in stock.

Does it place orders with my suppliers?

It drafts the purchase order. The order is placed only after you approve it.

How are suggested quantities worked out?

From selling speed, your supplier’s lead time and the days of cover you want to keep.

Can it stop overselling online?

Yes. Every channel draws from the same stock count, so the last item can’t be sold twice.

Can I set different cover for different products?

Yes. Set the cover you want per product or per collection.
