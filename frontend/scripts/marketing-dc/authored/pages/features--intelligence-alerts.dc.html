FAQs

Intelligence Alerts questions.

Related: Business Brain · Opportunity Radar · AI-Powered Insights

What can I set alerts on?

Product stock (days of cover), overdue customer credit and the margin gap between branches.

How often are watches checked?

Every hour. The morning briefing also summarises anything that changed overnight.

Will an alert do something automatically?

No. An alert can come with a prepared action, like a reorder or reminder, but it only runs after you approve it.

Will the same alert keep repeating?

No. A watch alerts once when it trips, then waits until the value recovers. You can also remove the watch or change its line.

Who receives alerts?

The owner and managers, in their Noxtill notifications, with a link straight to the finding.
