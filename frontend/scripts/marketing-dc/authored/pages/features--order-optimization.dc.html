FAQs

Order optimization questions.

Related: Automated Fulfillment · Smart Inventory · AI-Powered Operations

How does it pick the location for an order?

By who has all the items in stock, who is closest to the customer and what it costs to ship, weighted the way you choose.

Can I override where an order ships from?

Yes. Any order can be moved to another location by hand, and the reason is kept.

What is batched picking?

Orders going out from the same place are grouped into one pick list sorted by shelf, so staff collect everything in one pass.

Will it split an order across locations?

Only if you allow it. By default it prefers one location that can fill the whole order.

Does it work with my own delivery riders?

Yes. Orders routed to a location can go to your riders or to a shipping carrier.
