Better photos

Old notebooks welcome.

Faded ink, ruled pages and cramped handwriting are what this was built for. A few habits make every page read cleaner.

  1. Good light, no flash glareDaylight or an even lamp keeps faint ink readable.
  2. Whole page in the frameKeep all four corners in view so no column is cut off.
  3. One page per photoFor a book, photograph each page; for many pages, a PDF is easiest.

FAQs

Document scanning questions.

Related: Data Extraction · Review & Approve Data · Receipt & Invoice Scan

Can it read handwriting?

Yes. It is built for handwritten registers, ledgers and price lists as well as printed pages. Anything it is unsure of is marked for you to check.

Which files can I upload?

JPEG, PNG and WebP photos up to 10 MB, and PDFs up to 20 MB and 40 pages. HEIC photos and Word or Excel files are not supported yet.

Do I have to say what kind of page it is?

No. You can pick the kind of page, or choose “anything else” and let it sort each row on its own.

Is anything saved straight away?

No. Scanned data waits for your review and goes into your records only after you approve it.

Can I see the original later?

Yes. The original photo or PDF stays with the scan so you can compare it with what was read.
