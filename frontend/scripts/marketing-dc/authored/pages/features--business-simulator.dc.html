FAQs

Business Simulator questions.

Related: Digital Twin · Business Brain · Opportunity Radar

What can I change in a scenario?

Prices, discounts and promotions, staff hours and shifts, opening days and hours, and product costs.

Where do the projections come from?

From your own recent sales, costs and staffing in Noxtill, including how sales moved after past changes.

How accurate is it?

Every result is shown as a likely range with a confidence level. Short histories and unusual weeks lower the confidence.

Does running a scenario change anything?

No. Scenarios are sandboxed. You apply a change yourself in the module it belongs to when you are ready.

Can I share a scenario with my manager?

Yes. Saved scenarios can be opened by anyone on your team with access to Business Intelligence.
