What does it do on its own?

Routine store work inside your rules: syncing stock, adjusting prices within your limits, improving listings and drafting reorders and campaigns.

What needs my approval?

Anything beyond your limits, such as a big price change or a campaign above your auto-send size, waits for you.

Can I see everything it did?

Yes. Every action is in the operations log with what changed, when and why.

Can I turn a job off?

Yes. Switch any job off, or set it to draft only so nothing goes live without you.

Does it work with my existing store?

It runs on your Noxtill Website & Commerce store and connected channels.
