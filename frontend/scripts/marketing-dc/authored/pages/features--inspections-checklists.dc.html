Why it matters

A paper sheet can’t prove the job was done right.

Paper checklists get skipped, filled in from memory or lost in the van. When a customer questions a repair, you need to show what was checked, by whom, when, and what it looked like.

With Noxtill, the checklist travels with the work order. Answers, readings, photos and the signature are recorded on site and kept with the job.

Templates

Write it once. Publish it. Every visit follows it.

A template is a list of items, each with an answer type, whether it is required and whether it needs photo evidence. Publishing a new version supersedes the old one; jobs already started keep the version they began with.

FAQs

Inspection and checklist questions.

Related: Work Orders · Job Tracking · Technician Scheduling

What answer types can a checklist item have?

Pass/fail, yes/no, a number, free text, a photo or a signature. Each item can be required or optional and can ask for photo evidence.

What happens to jobs when I publish a new version?

New jobs use the new version. A job that already started keeps the version it began with, so its record matches what the technician actually saw.

Can technicians fill in a checklist without signal?

Yes. Answers, notes, the signature and status taps are queued on the device and sync when it reconnects. The office sees them as saved only after they sync.

Who approves an inspection result?

A manager or owner. Technicians record the answers; the result is approved by someone with Field Service manager access.

Can a failed item turn into more work?

Yes. A failed item prompts for a comment, a photo and a follow-up work order, and the repair can be quoted straight from the work order.
