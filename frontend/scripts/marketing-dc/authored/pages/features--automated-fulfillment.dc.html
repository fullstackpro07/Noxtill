Labels and packing slips

Packed means ready to go.

When an order is packed, its shipping label and packing slip are created from the order, with the carrier and service chosen by your rules. Print them together and drop the box at collection.

FAQs

Automated fulfillment questions.

Related: Order Optimization · Smart Inventory · Commerce Within Your Rules

What happens automatically after an order is paid?

It is routed to a location, added to a pick list, and gets its label and packing slip when packed. The customer gets an update at each step.

Which carriers can I use?

Your connected shipping carriers, or your own delivery riders for local orders.

Can I hold an order back?

Yes. Put any order on hold, for example to check an address, and it stops moving until you release it.

Do customers get tracking?

Yes. The shipped message includes the tracking link, followed by out-for-delivery and delivered updates.

Can I change the customer messages?

Yes. Each update uses your wording and can go by email or WhatsApp.
