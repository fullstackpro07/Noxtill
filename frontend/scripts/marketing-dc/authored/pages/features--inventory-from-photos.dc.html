FAQs

Inventory from photos questions.

Related: Product Digitization · Review & Approve Data · Smart Inventory

Can I photograph a handwritten count?

Yes. Handwritten and printed stock counts both work, as photos or PDFs.

Does a count create new products?

No. A stock count updates products you already have. Lines it can’t match wait for you to choose a product.

Will I see the changes first?

Yes. Every product shows what Noxtill had, what you counted and the difference, before you approve.

What if I miscounted one line?

Edit the number during review. Only the approved figures are saved.

Does it remember my short names?

Yes. When you match a written name to a product, it remembers it for the next count.
