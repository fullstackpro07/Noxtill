FAQs

Data extraction questions.

Related: Document Scanning · Review & Approve Data · Product Digitization

What does it extract from a page?

The rows and fields on it: names, phone numbers, emails, amounts, quantities, product codes, dates and notes, depending on the kind of page.

What happens when it can’t read something?

The field is highlighted with a lower confidence so you can correct it, and you can reprocess the page or retake the photo.

Does it check the numbers?

Yes. Values are checked as the kind of field they are, and totals that don’t match their lines are flagged.

Can I edit the extracted table?

Yes. Edit any cell or row before approving. The original page stays alongside for comparison.

Where does the data go?

Nowhere until you approve it. Then each row goes to customers, products, expenses, suppliers, credit balances or stock, as you choose.
