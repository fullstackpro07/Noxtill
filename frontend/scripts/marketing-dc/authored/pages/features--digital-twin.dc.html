What is a digital twin of a business?

A live model of how the parts of your business connect — branches, stock, staff, customers, suppliers and money — kept up to date from your own records.

Do I need to set it up?

No. It is built from the modules you already use, such as Sales, Inventory, Staff, Customers and Branches.

How is it different from the Business Simulator?

The twin is the model of your business as it is now. The Simulator uses that model to test a specific decision and compare scenarios.

Does exploring a change affect real data?

No. Changes you explore in the twin stay in the model until you act on them in the real module.

How current is the model?

It updates as records are saved, so sales, deliveries and shifts appear as they happen.
