FAQs

Receipt and invoice scan questions.

Related: Document Scanning · Data Extraction · Review & Approve Data

What does it read from an invoice?

The supplier, the date, each line with quantity and price, and the printed total.

What if the total doesn’t match the lines?

The scan is flagged with the difference and any line it couldn’t read, so you can check before approving.

Where do scanned receipts end up?

As expenses by default. You can send any row somewhere else, such as a supplier record, during review.

Can I keep the paper copy digitally?

Yes. The original photo or PDF stays attached to the scan.

Is anything posted automatically?

No. The expense is prepared for you and saved only after you approve it.
