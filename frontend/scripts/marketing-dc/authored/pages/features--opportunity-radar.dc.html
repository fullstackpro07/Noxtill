FAQs

Opportunity Radar questions.

Related: Business Brain · Intelligence Alerts · Business Simulator

Where do the opportunities come from?

From checks over your own sales, customers, credit, stock, reviews and deliveries in Noxtill. Nothing is pulled from outside data.

What does confidence mean?

How much data supports the signal and how long the pattern has held. Short or noisy patterns are marked lower.

Can I stop seeing something?

Yes. Dismiss it with a reason and it won’t be raised again for the same reason.

Does it take action on its own?

No. It shows the signal and the evidence. Any action is prepared for you and runs only after you approve it.

How is this different from Business Brain?

Business Brain is your daily briefing. Opportunity Radar is the full map of openings and risks, ranked by size and urgency.
