FAQs

Commerce rules questions.

Related: AI-Powered Operations · Dynamic Pricing · Approval Workflows

What can I set rules for?

Prices, discounts, stock orders, campaigns and new product listings, each with its own limits.

What happens when an action is over a limit?

It is prepared and sent to the person you chose to approve that kind of action. It doesn’t run until they do.

Can I make everything need approval?

Yes. Set any area to draft only and every action in it waits for a person.

Can I undo an automated action?

Yes. Actions in the log can be reversed, and the reversal is logged too.

Who can change the rules?

Only the owner and the people you give permission to manage commerce settings.
