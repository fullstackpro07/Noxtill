No duplicates

It learns what you call things.

Handwritten lists shorten names. When a line looks like a product you already sell, Noxtill suggests the match and lets you decide. Once you confirm, it remembers that name for next time.

  • “Capp. lrg”→Cappuccino · Large
  • “Almd crois”→Almond croissant
  • “Ban bread slice”→Banana bread
  • “Oat flat wht”→Flat white · Oat milk

After approval

Ready to sell the same day.

Approved products appear in Products and Services with their category and price, so you can ring them up at the counter and list them online without typing a single line.

  • New products are created; matched lines use the product you already have
  • Prices and categories are editable before you approve
  • Nothing changes in your catalogue until you approve

FAQs

Product digitization questions.

Related: Inventory from Photos · Review & Approve Data · Data Extraction

What can I turn into products?

Menus, printed or handwritten price lists, supplier catalogues and product sheets, as photos or PDFs.

Will it create duplicates of products I already have?

It suggests a match when a line looks like an existing product and lets you choose. Confirmed matches are remembered for next time.

Does it set the category?

It suggests one from the page, such as the menu heading, and you can change it before approving.

Can I fix a price before it goes live?

Yes. Every name, category and price can be edited during review.

When do the products appear in my catalogue?

Only after you approve them in the review step.
