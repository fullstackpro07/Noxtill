FAQs

Dynamic pricing questions.

Related: Commerce Within Your Rules · Smart Inventory · AI-Powered Operations

What makes a price change?

Demand, stock levels and your product costs, checked on the schedule you set.

Can a price ever go below my floor?

No. Prices always stay between your floor and ceiling and above your minimum margin.

What happens to big price changes?

Any change bigger than your auto-approve limit waits in your approval queue with the reason.

Will my channels show different prices?

No. The same price is kept in step across your online store, POS and connected channels.

Can I see what changed and why?

Yes. Every change is logged with the old price, the new price and the reason.
