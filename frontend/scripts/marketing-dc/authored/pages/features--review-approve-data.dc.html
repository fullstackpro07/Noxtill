FAQs

Review and approval questions.

Related: Data Extraction · Document Scanning · Inventory from Photos

Is anything saved before I approve?

No. Read data waits in review and is saved to your records only when you approve it.

Can I see the original while reviewing?

Yes. The original photo or PDF is shown beside the rows read from it.

Can I change where a row goes?

Yes. Each kind of page has a default destination, and you can change it for any single row.

What about records I already have?

Possible duplicates are shown side by side. Use the existing record, create a new one or skip the row.

Is there a quick way for clean pages?

Yes. Accept all clear rows at once and only work through the flagged ones.
