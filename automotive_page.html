Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about Noxtill for automotive businesses.

View all FAQs →
Can I manage both appointments and walk-ins? +

Yes. Walk-ins can be added straight into the day view alongside booked appointments.

Can I track individual vehicle service history? +

Every vehicle has its own record with past services, parts used and notes.

Do you support parts inventory management? +

Parts stock updates as service orders are completed, with low-stock alerts.

Do you integrate with payment providers? +

Noxtill connects with major card and online payment providers so invoices can be paid digitally.

Can I send WhatsApp updates to customers? +

Service status updates, reminders and follow-ups can all be sent automatically over WhatsApp.

Is Noxtill suitable for multi-location garages? +

Yes — run several locations from one account and compare their performance.

Is there a mobile app for my team? +

Technicians can view assigned jobs, update status and add notes from their phone.

Can I export my data and reports? +

Reports and records can be exported whenever you need them.
